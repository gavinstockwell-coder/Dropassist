# Day-before-drop checklist

**The night before (about 10 minutes)**

- [ ] Look at the week's drop list and update your **watchlist**: keywords, excludes, colors and sizes in priority order.
      Put your most-wanted item at the top.
- [ ] The **active profile** is filled and current: address, phone, email. The card is saved and not expired.
- [ ] **Dry run** on Supreme with a keyword that's in stock. Check that:
  - [ ] the right size was picked
  - [ ] every checkout field filled (the log says `Checkout filled: …` with nothing under `not found`)
  - [ ] the log shows `card: Visa •1234` (or your card), and the card boxes are filled
  - [ ] the beep and the notification came through
  - [ ] you click **Clear cart** afterwards
- [ ] Glance at the popup log for any `products-json not found` or `not found:` warnings. If you see one, fix the
      selector (README → "Updating selectors").

**Drop morning**

- [ ] Sign in to supreme.com in this browser, if you use an account.
- [ ] Close other heavy tabs. In Opera GX, turn off the RAM/CPU limiter.
- [ ] Turn off computer sleep and screen-saver lock for the next hour. Plug in your laptop.
- [ ] Turn off Do Not Disturb / Focus Assist so the notification and sound get through.
- [ ] Popup → **unlock the card vault** (🔒 → passphrase).
- [ ] Popup → **sync** the clock. The offset should be small (under a few hundred ms).
- [ ] **Arm next drop** by 10:50 AM ET (9:50 AM Central). Check that the popup countdown points at the right drop.
- [ ] Leave the site tab in front when it opens (about 1 minute before).

**When it alerts you**

- [ ] Confirm the card, solve any CAPTCHA, and check the item/size/total.
- [ ] Click the highlighted pay button yourself.
- [ ] Popup → **Stop** when you're done. Copy the log if something looked off.
